# Welcome to Ligature Docs

Ligature provides stateless, edge-rendered SVGs for your GitHub READMEs and personal websites. You can simply copy a Markdown URL to embed dynamic widgets anywhere.

## Why Ligature?

Unlike other widget providers that might be slow or require complex setup, Ligature is designed to be:
- **Stateless & Edge-Rendered:** Extremely fast rendering globally.
- **Highly Customizable:** Infinite themeability to perfectly match your brand or profile.
- **Interactive & Creative:** From standard stats to gaming arcade contribution graphs (like Pacman and Snake).
